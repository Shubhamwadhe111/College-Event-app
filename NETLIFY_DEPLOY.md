# 🚀 Deploy Your Nexus Events to Netlify (FREE)

## Quick 5-Minute Deployment

### Step 1: Go to Netlify
1. Open your browser and go to: **https://netlify.com**
2. Click "Sign up" (it's completely free)
3. Sign up with GitHub, Google, or email

### Step 2: Deploy Your Site
1. Look for the big area that says **"Want to deploy a new site without connecting to Git? Drag and drop your site output folder here"**
2. Open your file explorer and navigate to: `D:\SEM3\event-management-app\build`
3. **Drag the entire `build` folder** onto the Netlify website
4. Wait 30 seconds - your site will be deployed!

### Step 3: Get Your Custom URL
1. Netlify will give you a random URL like: `https://amazing-cupcake-123456.netlify.app`
2. Click **"Site settings"** 
3. Click **"Change site name"**
4. Enter a unique name like:
   - `nexus-college-events`
   - `nexus-event-platform`
   - `shubham-nexus-events`
   - `nexus-campus-events`
   - `nexus-events-2025`

### Step 4: Your Final URLs
After changing the name, your site will be available at:
- **Main Website:** `https://your-chosen-name.netlify.app`
- **Admin Portal:** `https://your-chosen-name.netlify.app/nexusadmin`
