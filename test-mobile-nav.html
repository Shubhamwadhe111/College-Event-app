Mobile Navigation Test

This is a test page to verify the mobile sidebar navigation is working correctly.

On mobile devices (screen width ≤ 768px), you should see a hamburger menu button in the top right.

Clicking it should open a sidebar that slides in from the right side with all 9 navigation options.

Instructions:

  1. Resize your browser window to mobile size (≤ 768px wide) or use mobile device
  2. Click the hamburger menu button (☰) in the top right
  3. Verify the sidebar slides in from the right
  4. Verify all 9 navigation options are visible
  5. Click the overlay or close button (✕) to close
